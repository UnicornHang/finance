## Brand & Style

This design system delivers an ultra-clean, clinical, and reassuring environment built for modern enterprise fintech and treasury operations. It rejects heavy drop shadows, decorative noise, and gradients in favor of pure flat surfaces, surgical hairline borders, and airy negative space. The visual character communicates absolute precision, institutional safety, and effortless clarity.

### Personality & Emotional Response
- **Surgical Precision:** Every border, metric, and baseline is aligned with mathematical rigor, fostering trust among financial controllers, treasurers, and institutional operators.
- **Invigorating Clarity:** Crisp, cool off-white canvases paired with luminous cyan-cerulean and mint accents produce a sense of clean momentum without eye strain during all-day usage.
- **Restraint & Decisiveness:** Eliminates all non-functional visual artifacts. Flat solid color blocks and precise typography convey confidence and speed.

### Design Movement
- **Architectural Minimalism:** Structured grid systems, strictly flat solid planes, hair-thin divider boundaries, and purposeful whitespace.
- **Zero-Gradient Standard:** Absolute prohibition of linear, radial, or mesh gradients. Color differentiation relies entirely on solid values, tint fills, and crisp contrasts.

## Colors

The color palette is built on strict solid planes of light, cool tone washes, and vibrant primary and secondary anchors. Gradients are prohibited across all layers, backgrounds, surfaces, and interactive states.

### Palette Architecture
- **Primary Accent (`#0284c7`):** Vibrant Cerulean Blue. Used for core interactive items, primary button fills, focused strokes, active tabs, and operational highlights.
- **Secondary / Success Accent (`#059669`):** Pure Crisp Jade. Signifies positive yields, inflow, verified compliance, and completed settlements. Paired with a solid tinted surface fill (`#ecfdf5`).
- **Alert / Destructive (`#dc2626`):** Solid Crimson. Reserved for balance shortfalls, compliance flags, and destructive account actions. Paired with a solid tinted surface fill (`#fef2f2`).
- **Warning (`#d97706`):** Solid Amber. Used for pending validations and escrow locks. Paired with a solid tinted surface fill (`#fffbeb`).
- **Neutral Canvas & Surfaces:**
  - Base Application Background: `#f8fafc` (Cool Slate Off-White, strictly flat).
  - Component / Card Surface: `#ffffff` (Pure Crisp White).
  - Secondary Card / Inset Surface: `#f1f5f9` (Subtle Flat Ice).
- **Text & Hierarchy:**
  - Primary Text: `#0f172a` (Crisp Charcoal Slate — high legibility, never harsh pure `#000000`).
  - Secondary Text: `#334155` (Mid Slate).
  - Tertiary / Subtext: `#64748b` (Soft Slate).
  - Disabled / Placeholder: `#94a3b8` (Muted Slate).
- **Structural Borders:**
  - Primary Structural Border: `#e2e8f0` (Crisp Neutral Border).
  - Hairline Subtle Border: `#f1f5f9`.
  - Focused Element Border: `#0284c7`.

## Typography

The typography system relies exclusively on Plus Jakarta Sans. Its contemporary geometric curves and balanced proportions offer high readability across dense financial data tables, KPI cards, and operational dashboards.

### Principles
- **Tabular Numerics:** All currency figures, rates, basis points, and ledgers must enable open-type tabular figures (`font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`) to preserve column alignment.
- **Micro-Copy Crispness:** Category labels, status headers, and table head cells rely on uppercase tracking (`letterSpacing: 0.02em` or `0.03em`) with tight semibold rendering.
- **Hierarchy Separation:** Visual hierarchy is communicated primarily through font size and crisp tone steps (`#0f172a` down to `#64748b`), avoiding overly heavy black weights that disrupt the light, clinical layout.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built on a 12-column fluid grid system pinned to a maximum canvas width of 1440px for desktop consoles, with full fluid adaptation across tablet and mobile displays.

### Breakpoints & Grid Rules
- **Desktop (>= 1280px):** 12 columns, 24px (`1.5rem`) gutters, 32px (`2rem`) outer margin. Maximum viewport content constrained to 1440px centered, or edge-to-edge for widescreen analytics tables.
- **Tablet (768px – 1279px):** 8 columns, 16px (`1rem`) gutters, 24px (`1.5rem`) outer margin. Side-by-side transaction modules collapse into vertically stacked 8-column blocks.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4 columns, 12px (`0.75rem`) gutters, 16px (`1rem`) outer margin. Action bars pin to bottom-fixed panels.

### Spacing Rhythms
- Internal component padding is strictly governed by `space-sm` (8px), `space-md` (16px), and `space-lg` (24px).
- Complex ledger rows use a constant height of 48px or 56px with horizontal cell padding locked at `space-md`.
- Layout sections keep a clean `space-xl` separation to guarantee high visual breathing room against the off-white background.

## Elevation & Depth

This design system deliberately eschews standard ambient blur shadows, diffuse drops, or multi-layered gray elevations. Depth is established through flat tonal stratification and crisp, hairline boundary rules.

### Flat Stratification Framework
- **Canvas Base (Level 0):** Flat `#f8fafc`. Continuous surface wash spanning the viewport.
- **Resting Surface (Level 1):** Flat pure white `#ffffff` enclosed by a solid 1px border of `#e2e8f0`. No box shadow is applied.
- **Layered Inset (Level 1-Sub):** Flat `#f1f5f9` within a white card container, producing crisp separation for sub-ledgers, breakdown panels, or search filters without border clutter.
- **Raised Interactive Surfaces (Level 2 - Popovers, Menus, Modals):** Pure `#ffffff` surface with a crisp 1px solid border of `#cbd5e1`, accompanied strictly by an ultra-crisp, non-diffuse, low-spread edge delineation: `0 4px 12px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.05)`. Muddy, heavy black or colored ambient glow shadows are strictly forbidden.
- **State Highlights:** Interactive elevation is conveyed through color shift: hover transitions move card borders from `#e2e8f0` to `#cbd5e1`, or active elements snap to a solid `#0284c7` border highlight.

## Shapes

The design system uses a soft, highly controlled corner geometry (`roundedness: 1`). This maintains structural architectural clarity while softening utility items for ergonomic daily interaction.

### Radius Specifications
- **Cards, Modals & Main Containers:** `rounded-lg` (8px / 0.5rem) with solid 1px perimeter borders.
- **Interactive Controls (Buttons, Text Inputs, Dropdowns):** `rounded` (4px / 0.25rem) to maintain sharp, tool-like precision.
- **Status Badges, Indicator Pills & Chips:** Fully pill-shaped (`rounded-full` / 9999px) to contrast with rectangular data cards.
- **Checkboxes:** `rounded` (3px) with a 1.5px solid border.
- **Radio Buttons:** `rounded-full` (circle) with centered solid disc when selected.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Button:** Solid fill `#0284c7`, text `#ffffff`, font weight 600. No gradient. Hover state transitions to solid `#0369a1`. Active state: `#075985`. Focus ring: 2px offset solid `#0284c7`.
- **Secondary Button:** Solid fill `#ffffff`, border 1px solid `#e2e8f0`, text `#0f172a`. Hover state: solid fill `#f8fafc`, border `#cbd5e1`.
- **Tertiary / Ghost Button:** Transparent background, no border, text `#334155`. Hover state: solid `#f1f5f9`, text `#0f172a`.
- **Destructive Button:** Solid fill `#dc2626`, text `#ffffff`. Hover state: `#b91c1c`.

### Status Badges & Pills
- **Success / Positive:** Solid fill `#ecfdf5`, border 1px solid `#a7f3d0`, text `#059669`, font weight 600, `rounded-full`.
- **Primary / Active:** Solid fill `#f0f9ff`, border 1px solid `#bae6fd`, text `#0284c7`, font weight 600, `rounded-full`.
- **Warning / Escrow:** Solid fill `#fffbeb`, border 1px solid `#fde68a`, text `#b45309`, font weight 600, `rounded-full`.
- **Neutral / Draft:** Solid fill `#f8fafc`, border 1px solid `#e2e8f0`, text `#475569`, font weight 600, `rounded-full`.

### Input Fields & Selects
- **Resting:** Solid `#ffffff` fill, 1px solid `#e2e8f0`, height 40px, font size 14px, text `#0f172a`, placeholder text `#94a3b8`, padding horizontal 12px, border-radius 4px.
- **Focus:** 1px solid `#0284c7`, outline 2px solid `#bae6fd` with 0px offset.
- **Error:** 1px solid `#dc2626`, outline 2px solid `#fecaca`.

### Data Cards & Panels
- **Structure:** Solid `#ffffff` background, 1px solid `#e2e8f0` border, `rounded-lg` (8px). Padding: 20px desktop, 16px mobile.
- **Header:** Bottom 1px solid `#f1f5f9` divider separating metric summaries from lower ledger bodies when dense data is rendered.

### Tables & Data Lists
- **Header Cell (`<th>`):** Height 36px, solid `#f8fafc` background, text 11px uppercase, font weight 600, letter spacing `0.03em`, text `#64748b`, border bottom 1px solid `#e2e8f0`.
- **Body Cell (`<td>`):** Height 48px, solid `#ffffff` background, border bottom 1px solid `#f1f5f9`, text 13px/14px `#0f172a`. Numeric values rendered with `tabular-nums`.
- **Row Hover:** Pure flat background shift to `#f8fafc`.

### Checkboxes & Radios
- **Unchecked:** Solid `#ffffff` fill, 1.5px solid `#cbd5e1`, 16px x 16px size.
- **Checked (Primary):** Solid `#0284c7` fill, 1.5px solid `#0284c7`, white crisp checkmark icon (stroke width 2px).
- **Radio Active:** Solid `#ffffff` fill, 1.5px solid `#0284c7` border with an inner solid circle of `#0284c7` (8px).

### Financial Metric Displays (KPIs)
- **Label:** 12px uppercase semibold `#64748b`.
- **Value:** 28px semibold `#0f172a`, tabular figures.
- **Delta Indicator:** Compact pill with jade `#059669` text on flat `#ecfdf5` background and 1px border `#a7f3d0`.
